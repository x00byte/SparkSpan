Read the numbers together

Aggregate generation and uncached prefill are wall-time rates. Cached prompt tokens are counted separately. Request prefill means and time to first token describe requests, not the rate of the whole pool.

A restarted engine resets its baseline. Missing or stale engines leave measurement gaps; incomplete aggregate rates are shown as unavailable. Changing capacity, models or cache state changes the workload and should be recorded before comparing recipes.

Maintenance checklist

  1. Enable draining for the backend being reserved.
  2. Wait for the gateway to report draining, no in-flight work and fresh native running / waiting counts of zero.
  3. Confirm at least two polling intervals and no direct clients or older gateways assigning work.
  4. Stop only that backend. Test on a separate port.
  5. Restore the intended model, verify readiness, then resume admission.

One six-slot backend in maintenance leaves six production slots. Queueing and latency can increase. An unreachable backend is not proof that it is drained.
